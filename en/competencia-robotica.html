FutBot · Teleoperated Category

COMRob 2026 FutBot Tournament

Design, build, and program teleoperated mobile robots to compete in robotic soccer matches, integrating mechanics, electronics, programming, wireless communication, and team strategy.

Date: October 27, 2026
Venue: Universidad Iberoamericana Mexico City

FutBot Registration

Teams

  • 2 to 5 team members.
  • Each participant must provide a valid official photo ID.
  • One mentor or technical advisor over 18 years old is permitted, but may not directly interact during official matches.
  • Team members must have designed, built, programmed, and/or integrated more than 51% of the robot, including hardware and software.

Robots

  • Maximum of 2 registered and competing robots per team.
  • Maximum weight per robot: 1.4 kg.
  • Maximum diameter and height: 22 cm.
  • Maximum ball-capture depth: 1.5 cm.
  • Each robot must include a stable and accessible carrying handle.

Main Technical Requirements

Category Teleoperated through wireless communication.
Game ball Golf balls.
Power Battery-powered only, with voltage no greater than 48 V DC or 25 V AC RMS.
Communication RF, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth may be used for teleoperation. Communication between robots is limited to the 2.4 GHz band with a maximum power of 100 mW EIRP.
Kicker A ball-kicking mechanism is permitted and its power may be verified during the competition.
Field 243 cm long × 182 cm wide.

Safety and Design

  • Sharp edges and pinch points are not permitted.
  • Lithium batteries must be stored and charged using appropriate safety measures.
  • Robots must include an emergency stop function.
  • Wiring and active components must remain protected.
  • Any potentially hazardous robot behavior must be reported to the Organizing Committee at least two weeks before the event.

Originality and Kits

  • Robotics kits are allowed if the soccer-specific component is developed by the team.
  • Soccer-specific hardware or software not created by the team may not be used.
  • External code may be used with proper credit; complete third-party solutions are not accepted.

During the Competition

  • The match schedule will indicate the time and field for each game.
  • Teams must be at the field 5 minutes before the match starts.
  • Robots must be certified before the first match and may undergo random inspections.
  • Human interference with robots, the field, or the ball is not permitted during play.
  • Referee decisions during the match are final.
  • Teams must compete fairly and avoid deliberate interference or damage.
Important
