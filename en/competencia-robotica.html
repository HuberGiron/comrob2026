COMRob 2026 FutBot Tournament

Join the robotic soccer competition with a team of teleoperated mobile robots, combining mechanical design, electronics, programming, wireless communication, and team strategy.

Date
October 27, 2026
Venue
IBERO Mexico City
Teams
2 to 5 members
FutBot Registration Download full call

Key information

  • Maximum of 2 robots per team.
  • Teleoperated category using wireless communication.
  • Golf balls are the official game balls.
  • One mentor or technical advisor is permitted per team.

Robot

  • Maximum weight: 1.4 kg.
  • Maximum diameter: 22 cm.
  • Maximum height: 22 cm.
  • The design must comply with the tournament safety requirements.
