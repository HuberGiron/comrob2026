About the talk

A talk by Abel Espinoza from ABB Robotics Mexico.

Abel Espinoza

Service Director at ABB Robotics Mexico

Biography

Abel Espinoza has 32 years of professional experience, including 26 years at ABB, where he has built an extensive career in industrial robotics and service management.

He holds a degree in Industrial Mechanical Engineering from Universidad Tecnológica de Puebla. He began his career at ABB as a service engineer and subsequently took on roles as a project engineer, Site Leader and Project Manager before becoming Service Manager.

His career combines technical knowledge, project management experience and leadership, providing a comprehensive perspective on operations and customer service in the robotics industry.
